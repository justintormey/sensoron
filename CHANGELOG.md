## [0.9.0] - 2026-08-25

The research-digest revision, driven by docs/research.md (a critical analysis Justin commissioned) and his four rulings on its recommendations. Scope NARROWS to camera-bearing wearables (supersedes the all-modalities ruling; the temperature-sensor example moves to informative section 13, Future scope). SESSION's transmit-to-verifiable-non-retention allowance is CUT: green states are strictly on-device, which makes the 24-hour packet-capture test absolute. Four states stay co-equal, and verifiability becomes structural via a two-tier conformance model: Tier 1 Interlocked (anything a bench proves: optics, timing, interlock, tamper) and Tier 2 Certified (accredited-lab verification of the retention rules against a pinned firmware version, with a signed registry entry; re-certification required on sensing-relevant firmware changes), modeled on the FCC Cyber Trust Mark's lab/administrator two-role structure. New requirement 6.7: machine-readable, signed, versioned data-path statement with QR/URL pointer and a mandatory invertibility analysis behind any derived-data claim. New section 9, Security considerations (what the lamp proves vs claims; threat model by adversary class; iSeeYou correctly framed as the reason 6.1 is a power-rail mandate; reconstruction risk per the MIT ambient-light-sensor and event-camera results; honest limits of indicator lights per CHI 2024/2026). New section 10, Deployment considerations (P3P/DNT history with verified dates; prospective status of the certification program; PA HB 2603 and GDPR alignment). Name treatment per Justin: the SensorOn Standard (SOS). All 12 load-bearing research citations were independently verified by a 12-agent workflow before entering the page; corrections caught: Samsung announced (not shipped) fall 2026; the Mac power-rail LED story is iSeeYou's recommendation, not documented Apple hardware; HB 2603 penalties are tiered up to $1,500 plus a criminal misdemeanor for individuals, pending in committee; MIT paper has four authors; Cyber Trust Mark program mid-rebuild. References expanded to 33. Breaking pre-1.0 scope/semantics changes, MINOR bump.

## [0.8.0] - 2026-08-24

IETF register pass, eight directives from Justin. (1) Demo lamp now steps the full cycle on tap: ambient, session, recording, off, back to ambient. (2) "Tells the room" became "tells the viewer". (3) All body-camera references removed, since v0.6.0 established their green buffering fails the replay test; AMBIENT's lineage is now the breathing standby light (the sleeping Mac's indicator); references renumbered 1-20 with Axon dropped. (4) "The pixels were never the point" removed. (5) Sections 1 and 2 rewritten as factual presentation for a new reader, no arguing with absent conversations; section 1 retitled Introduction. (6) Conformance language now carries the verbatim BCP 14 boilerplate (RFC 2119 + RFC 8174) and the document keyword is MUST/MUST NOT throughout, per IETF practice. (7) Negative capability framings flipped positive ("I can only wake"). (8) Aphorism fragments removed document-wide ("Software never gets a vote", "The encoding never matters", "One lamp, one job", "That is the whole project", "the lamp never lied", courtroom line, marketing jab, and the rest); descriptions stand on their own. Consumer register survives only in the lede and demo readout; the spec below is now uniformly technical.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-08-24

AMBIENT keeps nothing, per Justin ("Remove. It keeps nothing."). The derived-data retention allowance is removed from AMBIENT everywhere: demo Keeps line ("Nothing. It senses and forgets."), states-table cell ("Nothing. The measurement evaporates as it is read."), 6.5 AMBIENT (SHALL NOT retain anything: not media, not readings, not labels, not counts), and the conformance data-path check (storage inspection finds no artifact of anything sensed). The model is now maximally pure: green states never keep anything of any kind, and 6.5 RECORDING states the consequence outright: RECORDING is the only state in which anything the sensors read persists, in any form, at any rate. "Derived data" survives as audit vocabulary only, explicitly marked as not a retention license. Breaking pre-1.0 semantics change, MINOR bump.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-08-24

Buffered sensor inputs resolved, Justin's pick from three options: green buffers die unread. New "interpretation buffer" definition (the transient window interpretation physically requires; the buffer is the act of reading, not keeping) and a new 6.5 rule: AMBIENT/SESSION may hold one, bounded at 10 s or the interpretation minimum (provisional), destroyed as interpretation completes, with no hardware or software path to promotion or transmission. A promote-capable buffer fails the replay test for every moment it holds, so pre-event capture is RECORDING while armed. Deliberate divergence from Axon documented in the spec's own words: a body camera in buffering mode is one trigger away from a courtroom; under SensorOn that lamp is red. Conformance gains a buffer bench item (stated bound, post-interpretation forensic read, no promotion path, red-while-armed pre-event capture). Confirmed in passing: the lamp grammar does match shipped bodycam behavior (Axon buffering blinks green, Body 4 blinks red while recording), with SensorOn adding waveform separation on top of color.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-08-24

Sessions are temporal, per Justin's correction of v0.4.1's wrong turn. SESSION now persists NOTHING: not audio, not a transcript, not readings, not a log of the exchange; the v0.4.1 "first-party recording of the user" framing is removed. Only the consequences of a session's actions may persist (his example kept near-verbatim: "the switch may be turned from off to on a lamp"), because an effect on the world is not a replay of the moment. His rule, near-verbatim in 6.5: if ANY portion of a session is saved or persists beyond it, it was not a session, it was a RECORDING, and the lamp SHALL have been red. Sessions may now be self-invoked by pre-defined sensor thresholds (added to the AMBIENT wake list). Reconstructive-data definition extended: a high-rate trace of any sensed channel is that channel's transcript (a millisecond temperature log = the thermal transcript). New section 7, "A worked example: one temperature sensor," walks his full lifecycle (ambient once-per-minute → threshold → session at once-per-millisecond → red while capturing/transmitting → back to ambient), demonstrating the standard with no camera on a three-dollar part. Conformance item 5 now checks post-session storage for artifacts. Sections renumbered 7-12. Breaking pre-1.0 state-semantics change, MINOR bump.

## [0.4.1] - 2026-08-24

The stenographer's rule (Justin's ruling, his copy near-verbatim): a transcript is a recording, full stop; the encoding never matters, the read-back does. Added as a named definition in section 5, settling the transcript half of the former open question (the embedding invertibility edge remains open). Consequence made explicit in 6.5: SESSION's persisted interaction is called what it is, a recording of the user permitted because the user invoked it, and the moment it captures anyone else's words or actions the replay test applies and the lamp SHALL go red.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-08-24

Semantics rewrite per Justin's round-2 gut-check, five directives. (1) AMBIENT vs SESSION now distinguished by capability envelope, not vibes: new section 6.5 "State capabilities" defines AMBIENT as sensing without consequence (derived data only, no transmission of reconstructive data, no action except changing its own state; why it senses is deliberately out of scope) and SESSION as sensing with agency (may act, may transmit reconstructive data only to verifiably non-retaining compute, interaction may persist, surroundings still under AMBIENT rules). (3) Scope extended to ALL sensor modalities, worn or installed (new section 2): humidity, temperature, audio, video, chemical, radar, everything; non-adopters owe users the reason ("equipment that hides its sensing state has a name"). (4) De-pixeled throughout; pixels were never the point. (5) RECORDING redefined around persistence, not files: new section 5 Definitions (derived data per ISO/IEC 19944-1, reconstructive data, persistence, the replay test); RECORDING triggers whenever reconstructive data is stored OR transmitted without verifiable non-retention, indicator entering the state no later than persistence begins. Added: published data-path statement requirement, conformance item 5 (24-hour packet capture in AMBIENT, attested endpoints in SESSION), two new open questions (embedding/transcript boundary; installed-device night behavior). Sections renumbered 1-11; demo copy updated to the new claims. Breaking pre-1.0 state-semantics change, MINOR bump.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-08-24

Enforceable-spec rewrite, per Justin's gut-check ("reads more like a wish than a spec"). RFC 2119 conformance language throughout (SHALL / SHALL NOT / SHOULD / MAY); every requirement now carries a measurable value sourced from two research sweeps: chromaticity boundary equations (aviation green per 14 CFR 25.1397, signal red per SAE J578), 2 mm minimum aperture (Meta's daylight-driven 1 mm → 2 mm correction), 50 mcd intensity floor per CIE 127 Condition A with the 50-200 mcd sunlight band, 3:1 contrast floor, timing envelopes (AMBIENT 2-6 s period / trough ≥ 25% peak / edges ≥ 300 ms; SESSION ≤ 10% variation; RECORDING 1-2 Hz square at 40-60% duty with full extinction; global 2 flashes/s ceiling under WCAG 2.3.1's 3/s line), waveform redundancy so all states read with hue removed (Birch 2012, WCAG 1.4.1, ISO 9241-112), and a new section 5 bench-conformance checklist (colorimeter, photodiode, Blondel-Rey α = 0.2 s, interlock, tamper, hue-stripped glance test). Color discipline hardened: closed two-chromaticity set, mood/status/notification encoding on the sensor indicator prohibited (mood-ring open question eliminated per Justin). Provisional engineering-derived values are labeled as such on the page; INSIGHTS.md records primary vs secondary vs derived provenance. Demo brought into self-compliance (AMBIENT peak 50% of SESSION, trough 30% of peak; RECORDING off phase at full extinction) with per-state normative stamps in the readout. References section added (20 entries).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-24
